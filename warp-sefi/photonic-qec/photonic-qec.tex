\section*{Abstract}

Photonic quantum systems suffer from loss, dephasing, mode mixing, and spectral drift.
Conventional photonic quantum error correction (QEC) encodes information in bosonic
codes or multi-photon entangled states, but these approaches lack a geometric principle
governing error formation and correction. SEFI/GWFM provides such a principle by
defining stability surfaces, geometric identity layers, and worldline-based field emergence.
This manuscript develops a geometric framework for photonic QEC based on SEFI/GWFM,
including stability surfaces, photonic error geometry, geometric stabilizers, syndrome
geometry, SPDC geometry, and CHSH geometry. The resulting structure provides a
physically grounded foundation for photonic QEC.

Photonic quantum information processing offers long coherence times, room-temperature

unique error channels:

\begin{itemize}
    \item photon loss,

    \item mode mixing,
    \item spectral wandering.

SEFI/GWFM provides a geometric foundation for photonic QEC by defining identity layers,
worldline geometry, and stability surfaces. Errors correspond to geometric displacements
off these surfaces, and correction corresponds to restoring geometric sovereignty.

This manuscript develops photonic QEC from SEFI/GWFM.

\[

Stability surfaces provide a geometric interpretation of photonic error channels.
