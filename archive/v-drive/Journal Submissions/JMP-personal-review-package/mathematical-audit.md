# Mathematical correction and provenance audit

Prepared October 11, 2026. This is an author-review companion, not a scientific supplement or a claim of independent peer review. The consolidated manuscript is a conditional classical model. The available PDFs do not support a submission claiming an established unified electron field theory.

All pages were text-extracted and rendered for inspection: GWFM, 5 pages; SEFI, 4 pages; DEFI, 7 pages. The selected DEFI file is version `(3)` from the last three-file upload, not the earlier `(2)` file. DNAmanuscript.pdf was outside the requested scope. Original PDFs were left unchanged.

## New conditional mathematics in the revision

The internal equations, direct Origin-energy proof, energy-exchange equation, equilibrium root equation, Hessian Schur complement, forced-oscillator solutions, completion of squares, and acceleration-zero counterexample are derived from the displayed revised assumptions. These are not attributed as already established findings of the original PDFs. They require author validation and have not received independent peer review. They use elementary variational and oscillator methods; their inclusion is not a claim of fundamental novelty.

The positive-Hessian result is only for internal variables on prescribed constant geometry. It does not establish coupled stability. The negative quartic terms after minimizing over internal coordinates demonstrate lack of geometric coercivity in that potential, not an unconditional theorem that the constrained full Hamiltonian is unbounded. Ostrogradsky applicability requires degeneracy and gauge-constraint analysis.

4. Prove existence of full coupled solutions realizing candidate constant-invariant internal equilibria.
5. Define the allowed sovereignty/warp transformations and check action invariance and observable meaning.
6. Supply any quantum spinor, gauge, statistical, amplitude, and detector constructions before claiming electron/QFT equivalence.
7. Review novelty against geometric particle actions with rigidity, torsion, and internal variables. The present reference checks are not an exhaustive priority search.

## Attribution and reference handling

The three original works are cited as author-supplied manuscripts with their author-reported JMP submission identifiers and unverified publication status. Published worldline references were checked against primary arXiv records; Woodard's review against its primary record and Scholarpedia; Noether's translation against its primary arXiv record (which corrects the source's page range to 186–207); and Feynman's positron paper against the APS record. Original SEFI canon, engine documentation, unified-theory manuscript, and an entry consisting only of the journal name were not promoted into verified scientific references. The source DEFI's misaligned citations (Frenet geometry citing Noether, oscillator statements citing unrelated entries, and unresolved markers) were not copied. Published, publicly posted, or accepted versions of the source documents should replace provisional references once identified.
