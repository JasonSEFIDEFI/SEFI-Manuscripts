Re: Proposed replacement manuscript for ADV26-AR-05694

Dear Editors,

I request permission to replace the manuscript currently associated with ADV26-AR-05694, "Coherent
Saddle Encoding and Displacement Recovery: Exact Conditions and Limitations," with the enclosed
consolidated manuscript, "Coherent Saddle Encoding and Displacement Recovery: Residual-State
Criteria and Timing Limits." This proposed replacement is intended to supersede the earlier files
under the existing tracking number, rather than constitute a separate parallel submission.

The replacement integrates the original coherent saddle construction with the complementary math-
ematical developments prepared under the working title "Conjugate Phase Geometry and Coher-
ent Recovery." Shared material is retained as the same construction, not presented as independent
new evidence. The main text now gives a general residual-state criterion for exact logical recovery
under label-preserving random-unitary noise, an exact entanglement-fidelity deficit, and a sufficient
channel-distance estimate. It develops the complete two-oscillator encoder, its coherent inverse, exact
common-translation recovery, and the reference-entanglement criterion.

The arbitrary-time analysis supplies a Gaussian dephasing law and a quadratic timing-error coeffi-
cient. The manuscript now makes explicit that this law assumes an encoder and its matched coherent
inverse. A nominal forward evolution with half-period phase compensation is a distinct off-time oper-
ation. Independent full-state calculations check that distinction, as well as the residual-state formulas,
nonzero-mean and singular-covariance noise, and preservation of reference information. Eight exe-
cutable numerical scripts and recorded outputs accompany the replacement.

To keep the recovery argument focused while retaining the supporting mathematics, the constrained
scalar-field, internal-response, localization, coherent-history, trajectory, and conditional geomet-
ric/conservation calculations appear in labeled appendices. Their assumptions remain separate; they
are not asserted to supply a common microscopic derivation of the quantum register. Explicit error
channels, matched flat and conventional controls, and implementation limitations are retained.

For continuity, this work also follows the declined submission ADV26-AR-04574, "Geometric Photonic
Quantum Error Correction via SEFI, GWFM, and DEFI." The replacement addresses its central
concern by specifying quantum channels and logical/reference recovery rather than inferring correction
from classical geometric restoration.

A change summary and source-to-result integration map identify the consolidation. Please advise
whether the proposed replacement is permitted at the current editorial stage and how the updated
files should be provided. No claim of publication acceptance, experimental validation, or intrinsic
curvature advantage is made.

Thank you for your consideration.
