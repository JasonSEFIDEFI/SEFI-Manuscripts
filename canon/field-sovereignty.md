Where FIELD ORIGIN defines primitive identity and FIELD AUTHORSHIP defines expressive identity, FIELD SOVEREIGNTY defines identity preservation — the rules that ensure the entity remains itself under all allowed transformations.

Sovereignty is the layer that protects identity.

identity invariance

allowed transformation groups

boundary conditions for warp

Sovereignty is the “identity guardrail” of SEFI.
