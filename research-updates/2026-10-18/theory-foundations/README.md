## Editorial and scientific choices

The document treats GWFM, SEFI, and DEFI as related roles within one proposed program, rather than treating each as an established theory. It retains the author's four layer names and the single-entity motivation. Independent identity coordinates, dimensional parameters, a regular timelike domain, and the action are explicit modeling assumptions. The Reality layer is acknowledged without inventing a fifth oscillator. AI, photonic, quantum, and cosmological applications remain future extensions.

The coupled action, first integral, internal Hessian result, square-completion limitation, and oscillator counterexample use the conditional mathematical realization developed in the previous author-review draft. They are not claimed as experimentally validated laws, a full well-posedness theorem, or a new derivation of the electron field. The general geometric equation is a variational statement, not an expanded constrained initial-value solution. Independent author validation remains necessary, as does a fuller novelty review.

Rewriting from first principles does not erase publication history. No email was sent, no manuscript submitted, and no prior submission changed.
