\section{Toy Model: Single-Entity Reality Field in FRW Spacetime}

We construct a minimal cosmological model in which a single continuous worldline
$\gamma$ generates a reality field $R(x)$ over an expanding Friedmann--Robertson--Walker
(FRW) spacetime. This toy model demonstrates explicitly how a one-entity field
naturally produces the density scaling associated with early-universe expansion.

\subsection{Spacetime and Scale Factor}

Consider a spatially flat FRW spacetime with metric
\begin{equation}
ds^2 = -dt^2 + a(t)^2\, d\vec{x}^{\,2},
\end{equation}
where $a(t)$ is the cosmological scale factor. Let the manifold be $M$ with coordinates
$(t,\vec{x})$.

\subsection{Single Entity Worldline}

Define the entity's worldline as a mapping
\begin{equation}
\gamma : \tau \mapsto M, \qquad
\gamma(\tau) = \big(t(\tau), \vec{x}(\tau)\big).
\end{equation}

For the toy model, assume:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Monotonic time: $t(\tau) = \tau$.
\item Comoving exploration: the entity spans comoving positions $\vec{\chi}$ with
\begin{equation}
\vec{x}(\tau,\vec{\chi}) = a(\tau)\,\vec{\chi}.
\end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

Thus $\gamma$ represents a continuous worldline structure whose segments explore
comoving space as the universe expands.

\subsection{Presence Kernel and Reality Field}

Define a presence kernel
\begin{equation}
K\big(x,\gamma(\tau,\vec{\chi})\big)
= \delta\!\big(t - \tau\big)\,
  \delta^{(3)}\!\big(\vec{x} - a(\tau)\vec{\chi}\big),
\end{equation}
and take a constant identity density $\mathcal{I}(\tau,\vec{\chi}) = I_0$.

The reality field is then
\begin{equation}
R(t,\vec{x})
= \int d\tau \int d^3\chi \,
K\big(x,\gamma(\tau,\vec{\chi})\big)\,
\mathcal{I}(\tau,\vec{\chi}).
\end{equation}

Substituting the kernel,
\begin{equation}
R(t,\vec{x})
= I_0 \int d^3\chi \,
\delta^{(3)}\!\big(\vec{x} - a(t)\vec{\chi}\big).
\end{equation}

Change variables from $\vec{\chi}$ to $\vec{x}$ using
\begin{equation}
\vec{x} = a(t)\vec{\chi}
\qquad\Rightarrow\qquad
d^3\chi = \frac{d^3x}{a(t)^3}.
\end{equation}

Thus,
\begin{equation}
R(t,\vec{x})
= I_0 \int \frac{d^3x'}{a(t)^3}\,
\delta^{(3)}\!\big(\vec{x} - \vec{x}'\big)
= \frac{I_0}{a(t)^3}.
\end{equation}

Hence the single-entity reality field dilutes as
\begin{equation}
R(t,\vec{x}) \propto a(t)^{-3},
\end{equation}
matching the standard cosmological scaling of number density in an expanding universe.

\subsection{Electron--Positron Symmetry from Time Orientation}

In the GWFM framework, forward-time segments ($dt/d\tau > 0$) correspond to electrons,
while backward-time segments ($dt/d\tau < 0$) correspond to positrons. Define
\begin{equation}
R_e(t,\vec{x}) = \frac{\alpha I_0}{a(t)^3}, \qquad
R_p(t,\vec{x}) = \frac{(1-\alpha) I_0}{a(t)^3},
\end{equation}
with $0 \le \alpha \le 1$ encoding the fraction of forward- versus backward-oriented
segments. The total field is
\begin{equation}
R(t,\vec{x}) = R_e(t,\vec{x}) + R_p(t,\vec{x})
= \frac{I_0}{a(t)^3}.
\end{equation}

Early-universe electron--positron symmetry corresponds to $\alpha \approx \tfrac{1}{2}$.

\subsection{Inflation as Sovereignty Saturation}

In SEFI, the sovereignty constraint requires
\begin{equation}
\left\| \frac{d^2 r}{dt^2} \right\| \le S_0,
\end{equation}
where $r(t)$ is the spatial part of the worldline.

For comoving exploration,
\begin{equation}
r(t) = a(t)\vec{\chi},
\qquad
\frac{d^2 r}{dt^2} = \ddot{a}(t)\vec{\chi}.
\end{equation}

Inflation corresponds to the regime
\begin{equation}
\left\| \ddot{a}(t)\vec{\chi} \right\| \approx S_0,
\end{equation}
i.e.\ the expansion rate saturates the sovereignty bound. This provides a geometric
interpretation of inflation as maximal allowed worldline expansion.
