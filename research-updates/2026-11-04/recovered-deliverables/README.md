# Recovered recent research deliverables

Archived 4 November 2026 at Jason Duran Dutton's request to synchronize research.
Folders retain the date of the original local delivery. Prior manuscripts, figures,
prospectuses, educational materials and videos are preserved as historical artifacts.
They have not been newly scientifically validated or promoted by this synchronization.
The current computing study explicitly separates established mathematics from physical
interpretation and untested applications. Earlier language must be read in its dated context.

Duplicate byte-identical packages are retained once. Complete graphical-abstract source,
corrected video, advisor learning materials and prospectuses are included where missing
from the compared Git trees. Compilation intermediates and redundant deployment builds
are not research source deliverables. Private school correspondence/admissions packages
and job materials are outside this archive.
