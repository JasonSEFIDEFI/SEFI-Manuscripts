\opening{Dear Editors,}

Thank you for taking the time to review my manuscript,
\textit{``Displacement-Engineered Warp Fields: Stability Surfaces and Worldline Geometry.''}

In preparing this revised submission, I have removed all references to
``crafts,'' ``tic-tacs,'' or any terminology associated with public
aerospace reports. The manuscript has been rewritten strictly from a
scientific and geometric-field-theoretic perspective, with all discussion
grounded in established physics, differential geometry, and worldline
formalisms.

The revised version focuses solely on the mathematical framework,
stability analysis, and geometric operators relevant to displacement-driven
field configurations. No speculative or non-scientific language remains.
All sections have been tightened for clarity, rigor, and alignment with
AIP Advances review standards.

I sincerely appreciate the time and effort the editorial team and
reviewers have invested in evaluating this work. Thank you for considering
this revised manuscript for publication.

\closing{Sincerely,}
