# Narrated graphical abstract

Final duration: 81.600 seconds. All ten supplied recordings are used once in numeric order. Speech is not time-stretched. Processing: ten millisecond boundary fades, at most trailing silence trimming after the recognized speech plus half a second, short pauses between clips, and a single overall gain adjustment of -0.94 decibels to retain one decibel of peak headroom. No equalization, noise reduction, music, or dynamic compression was applied.

The master is a twenty-four-bit mono wave file at forty-eight thousand samples per second. The video contains compressed narration and optional English subtitles. Subtitles use automatic speech alignment and should be checked against the recording. Original studio files remain unchanged in the sibling audio folder and are not republished in this package.

The mathematical figures remain candidate illustrations with proposed parameters, not experimental validation. The original silent video is retained separately.
