% -------------------------------------------------
\section*{Abstract}

SEFI:DNA extends the Single Entity Field Interpretation (SEFI) by introducing a geometric
framework for multi-entity identity. While SEFI Space defines a four-dimensional identity
manifold for a single entity, SEFI:DNA constructs a tensor product identity space capable of
representing coupled identity trajectories, identity entanglement, and multi-entity stability.
The resulting structure provides a geometric interpretation of multi-particle identity,
entanglement, and transformation compatible with worldline quantum field theory and
SEFI/GWFM geometry.

\vspace{0.5cm}

% -------------------------------------------------
% 1 INTRODUCTION
% -------------------------------------------------
\section{Introduction}

SEFI Space defines identity geometry for a single underlying entity using four basis
directions: FIELD ORIGIN, FIELD AUTHORSHIP, FIELD SOVEREIGNTY, and WARP
EXPRESSION. The identity state evolves as a trajectory in SEFI Space while the worldline
traverses spacetime.

Multi-entity systems require a generalization of this identity geometry. SEFI:DNA
introduces a multi-dimensional identity space constructed from tensor products of SEFI
Space, allowing identity coupling, identity entanglement, and multi-entity sovereignty
constraints.

The goals of this manuscript are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item To define SEFI:DNA as a tensor product identity space for multi-entity systems.
    \item To introduce identity entanglement and coupled identity trajectories.
    \item To define multi-entity sovereignty and warp constraints.
    \item To couple SEFI:DNA to worldline geometry in a manner compatible with SEFI/GWFM.
\end{itemize}

% -------------------------------------------------
% 2 MULTI-ENTITY IDENTITY SPACE
% -------------------------------------------------
\section{Multi-Entity Identity Space}

Let SS denote SEFI Space for a single entity. For a system of two entities, we define the
tensor product identity space



\[
SS_{2} = SS \otimes SS.
\]



An identity state in $SS_{2}$ is represented by



\[
\mathbf{I}_{2}(t) = \mathbf{I}_{1}(t) \otimes \mathbf{I}_{2}(t),
\]



where each $\mathbf{I}_{i}(t)$ is a SEFI Space identity vector.

For $N$ entities, the identity space becomes



\[
SS_{N} = SS^{\otimes N}.
\]



Identity coordinates for each entity combine to form a multi-entity identity tensor.

% -------------------------------------------------
% 3 IDENTITY ENTANGLEMENT
% -------------------------------------------------
\section{Identity Entanglement}

Identity entanglement occurs when the identity state cannot be written as a product of
individual identity vectors:



\[
\mathbf{I}_{2}(t) \neq \mathbf{I}_{1}(t) \otimes \mathbf{I}_{2}(t).
\]



Entanglement corresponds to coupled identity trajectories in SEFI:DNA. These coupled
trajectories represent geometric identity correlation between entities.

Identity entanglement may arise from:

\begin{itemize}
    \item geometric overlap of worldlines,
    \item shared sovereignty constraints,
    \item coupled warp transformations,
    \item multi-entity stability surfaces.
\end{itemize}

% -------------------------------------------------
% 4 MULTI-ENTITY SOVEREIGNTY
% -------------------------------------------------
\section{Multi-Entity Sovereignty}

Sovereignty generalizes to multi-entity systems by requiring invariance of the identity tensor
under allowed multi-entity transformations $T$:



\[
\mathbf{I}_{N}(t) = T(\mathbf{I}_{N}(t)).
\]



The allowed transformations form a sovereignty-preserving subgroup of the full
transformation group acting on the multi-entity worldline geometry.

Multi-entity sovereignty defines stability surfaces in $SS_{N}$, and errors correspond to
displacements orthogonal to these surfaces.

% -------------------------------------------------
% 5 MULTI-ENTITY WARP
% -------------------------------------------------
\section{Multi-Entity Warp}

Warp operations generalize to multi-entity systems by acting on each identity coordinate
and on coupled identity components.

A multi-entity warp operator $W_{N}$ acts on the identity tensor as



\[
\mathbf{I}'_{N}(t) = W_{N}(\mathbf{I}_{N}(t)).
\]



Coupled warp operations may induce correlated identity transformations, producing motion
in $SS_{N}$ along multi-axis directions.

Warp must satisfy multi-entity sovereignty constraints to preserve identity.

% -------------------------------------------------
% 6 COUPLING TO WORLDLINE GEOMETRY
% -------------------------------------------------
\section{Coupling to Worldline Geometry}

Identity coordinates in SEFI:DNA are constructed from multi-entity worldline geometry.
For two entities with worldlines $r_{1}(t)$ and $r_{2}(t)$, identity coordinates may be defined
as



\[
I_{O}^{(1)}(t) = O(r_{1}(t)), \quad I_{O}^{(2)}(t) = O(r_{2}(t)),
\]



and similarly for authorship, sovereignty, warp, and reality-layer functions.

Coupled worldline geometry induces identity entanglement and multi-entity warp
interactions.

\section{Discussion and Outlook}

SEFI:DNA provides a geometric identity framework for multi-entity systems. By defining
identity tensors, identity entanglement, multi-entity sovereignty, and multi-entity warp, the
framework extends SEFI Space to systems with multiple interacting entities.

Future work will focus on:

\begin{itemize}
    \item deriving explicit multi-entity layer functions,
    \item exploring identity geodesics in $SS_{N}$,
    \item defining multi-entity stability surfaces,
    \item investigating applications to quantum error correction and entanglement geometry.
\end{itemize}
